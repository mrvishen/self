About

Most of what I do is build small devices that read the world, make a decision and pass it on. I work with Arduino, ESP32 and Raspberry Pi, simulate circuits in PSpice, lay out boards in KiCad and write firmware in C and C++. I use Python for data work.

I also design interfaces in Figma, because a device nobody can use is only half finished. I co-founded Kshitij Labs, a freelancing and media agency.

My long-term goal is standardized, interoperable hardware that makes devices easy to connect, the way USB did for peripherals. Outside engineering I am a Shotokan karate black belt, a national-level player and a certified judge with the Karate India Organisation. I ride and I read a lot.
